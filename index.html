When to Go

A year in Ladakh

Ladakh has two faces. In summer the passes open and the valleys turn green with barley. In winter the land goes quiet, rivers freeze, and the snow leopard comes down from the ridges.

  1. Jan – Feb

    Deep Winter

    The Chadar trek, snow-leopard tracking in Hemis National Park, and frozen lakes. Expect −20 °C nights.

  2. Mar – Apr

    The Thaw

    Apricot blossom in Sham valley and empty monasteries. Some high passes remain shut.

  3. May – Sep

    High Season

    All roads open, festivals at Hemis and in the villages, and lakes at their bluest. Our recommended window.

  4. Oct – Dec

    Golden Autumn

    Poplar groves turn gold, the skies are at their clearest and the crowds are gone. Ideal for photographers.
